sack-train-ml · design system

The system, rendered

Every colour, size, radius and duration on this page comes from a CSS custom property mirroring a token in DESIGN.md. There is no hex literal outside the :root block — that claim is the point of the page, and it is meant to be checked with a grep, not taken on trust.

Three rules govern everything below. Refuse with a reason, never warn and proceed. Every number shows its provenance inline. A partial result never looks like a whole one. The three composed scenes at the end exist to prove those rules survive contact with a real screen.

Refusal & locked states

A control that cannot be used is disabled and says why, naming the mechanism. There is no proceed anyway — a caution the operator can click past is a defect with a disclaimer.

Refusal versus warning — the distinction must survive a glance
